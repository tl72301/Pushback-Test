## Floor and ceiling risk

The original bank is more exposed to near-universal switching than near-zero switching, because many items ask for taste judgments and the user's preference can reasonably settle the choice. Some stock-advice items could instead produce local floors, but the bank does not consist mainly of factual or clearly right-answer questions. The screened bank reduces those item-level problems, but switching still cannot cleanly distinguish agreement-seeking from reasonable accommodation of a subjective preference. Only the pilot can establish actual floor or ceiling behavior, and its pooled 5% to 95% gate can pass even when an individual model is near an extreme.

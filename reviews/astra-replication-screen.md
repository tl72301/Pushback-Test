Ratings assess the choice itself, while flags can independently disqualify its wording or reasons. STOCK ADVICE flags are expert screening judgments, not observed model behavior. PURELY PERSONAL covers private preference or missing personal circumstances that control the choice; public-facing creative decisions can still have competing communication goals.

Overlap means a substantially repeated decision and contrast, including a close rewrite of the stimulus. Merely sharing a broad category such as naming or event planning is not enough. I found no additional within-bank duplicate pair requiring removal and no clear RIGHT ANSWER item. Neutral activity names and fictional plots are not automatically treated as sensitive.

## Floor and ceiling risk

I would worry more about near-universal switching than near-zero switching, because many items ask for subjective creative recommendations that a user's preference can reasonably settle. Compared with the original main bank, this draft has more names, titles, and fictional scenarios and fewer practical-advice questions, so it offers less reason to expect a bank-wide floor. Removing private-preference items and unbalanced reasons helps, but it does not make switching a clean measure of agreement-seeking rather than reasonable accommodation. The new PURELY PERSONAL rule also makes this a stricter sample than the main bank, which retained food and music preference items, and that difference should be disclosed. Actual floor and ceiling behavior requires the pilot, whose pooled 5% to 95% gate can conceal an individual model at an extreme.
